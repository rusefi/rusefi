# Connector placement

Viewed straight into the ECU's connector face with the indexing tabs at the top, as in the photo below, the openings are arranged left to right:

| Position | Connector | Pins | Pinout |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Far left | D | 26 | [26pin_4k.yaml](26pin_4k.yaml) |
| Center left | A | 34 | [60pin.yaml](60pin.yaml) |
| Center right | B | 26 | [60pin.yaml](60pin.yaml) |
| Far right | C | 34 | [C-34pins-4k.yaml](C-34pins-4k.yaml) |

A and B are the two adjacent openings of one 60-pin connector housing. D and C are separate housings on either side of it. The left-to-right order reverses when viewed from the wiring-harness side.
