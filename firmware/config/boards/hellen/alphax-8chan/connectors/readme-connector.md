# Connector placement

Viewed straight into the ECU connector face with the indexing tabs at the top, as in the photo below, the openings are arranged left to right:

| Position | Connector | Pins | Pinout |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Far left | A | 34 | [8chan-A-B.yaml](8chan-A-B.yaml) |
| Left center | B | 26 | [8chan-A-B.yaml](8chan-A-B.yaml) |
| Right center | C | 34 | [8chan-C.yaml](8chan-C.yaml) |
| Far right | D | 26 | [8chan-D.yaml](8chan-D.yaml) |

A and B are adjacent openings in one 60-pin housing. C and D are separate housings to its right. The mating-plug view is mirrored.
