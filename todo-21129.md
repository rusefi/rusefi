## Knock (PRIORITY - in progress)

- Board inputs exist: AA3 = "Knock sensor +", AB3 = "Knock sensor ground"
  (connectors/m74_9.yaml).
- RESOLVED 2026-08-17: the knock pin is PA0 = ADC1 IN0, not PF7. AA3 goes into
  the onboard SGM321/LM321 ("3218K") conditioner, its OUT pin is AC-coupled to
  PA0 (idles ~1.76 V). Traced on the board with a multimeter and confirmed by
  the knocktest/knockpin diagnostics.
- BENCH VERIFIED 2026-08-17 (after the divider + steal fixes):
  - knocktest: bursts=200/200, real rate ~155 kHz (expected ~160 kHz);
  - PA0 idle: rms ~2180 (1.76 V), p2p ~50 counts (~40 mV) - quiet bias;
  - ADCD1 is ~100% ACTIVE (continuous background chain) - expected, knock
    windows steal it (verified mechanism, the steal itself only fires with
    spark events on a running engine).
  - Still to do on the bench: tap/click AA3 during knocktest, PA0 p2p must
    jump while PA1..PA3 stay flat.
- ADC1 is SHARED between knock and the slow sampling (EFI_SLOW_ADC ==
  KNOCK_ADC == ADCD1). Contract implemented in stm32_adc_v2.cpp +
  software_knock.cpp:
  - portInitAdc starts ADCD1 exactly once (runtime pointer compare, a second
    adcStart would re-allocate the DMA stream and brick the boot);
  - a knock window STEALS the ADC from the slow background chain
    (adcStopConversionI + adcStartConversionI under osalSysLock);
  - knockCompletionCallback resumes the chain (re-runs the aborted batch);
  - ADCD3 (EFI_ADC3_SLOW, CLT/IAT on F-port pins) is started unconditionally
    and has no other users now.
- ADC clock: Artery ADCCLK = HCLK/16/adcdiv with adcdiv in ADCCOM.cctrl
  [19:16]. mcuconf STM32_ADC_ADCPRE = DIV4 -> adcdiv 1 -> measured ~155 kHz
  knock sample rate (was ~6.8 kHz with the old DIV6 mapping). DIV2 fails the
  LLD compile-time range check, do not try.
- 21129 knock: 82 mm bore -> ~7.0 kHz (resonant sensors), knockFrequency = 7000,
  double frequency = OFF. Window 20..65 deg ATDC is only a starting point;
  calibrate knockBaseNoise/gains afterwards.
- Until knock is calibrated there is no detonation protection: on RON95 keep
  margin, if pinging is heard pull 1-2 deg in the WOT rows at 2900-4700 rpm
  of both ignition maps.

## Cranking / idle review (DONE 2026-08-17 in 21129.msq)

Reference: chiptuner.ru M74.8 diagnostic table (idle 840+-40 rpm, УОЗ 9+-5 deg,
MAP 0.38-0.41 bar, fill 115-125 mg/cycle, TPS 1-4%) - close enough for the
16V 21129.

- Cranking fuel math verified in fuel_math.cpp:
  crankingFuel = crankingCycleBaseFuel[mg] x crankingFuelCoef(CLT) x
  crankingTpsCoef(TPS). Tune: 20 mg x (2.8@-20C -> 1.0@65C+) x (3.0@0% TPS,
  flood-clear direction). Values were cross-checked against observed pulse
  widths earlier - left as-is.
- crankingTimingAngle 5 + crankingAdvance 0->15 deg (0-1000 rpm) = ~9 deg at
  300 rpm. OK.
- postCrankingFactor was 1.0 flat (no afterstart taper) - FIXED: 6x6 taper vs
  (engine revs x CLT): 2.2x at -20C decaying to 1.0 over ~200 revs, 1.0 flat
  when warm. Bins fixed to 0 40 80 120 160 200 revs.
- Idle timing: the converted stock base map has ~25-30 deg in the idle cell
  (800-1000 rpm / 35-40 kPa) - in the stock ECU the idle governor pulls it
  down to ~9-14 deg. FIXED: useSeparateAdvanceForIdle = enabled +
  idleAdvanceBins 0..2000 rpm / idleAdvance 16 15 13 12 11 10 10 12 deg
  (anti-stall slope, blends to the normal table at 2.5-5% TPS).
- Warm idle target 900 rpm (stock 840, tune's choice with AC/fan adders)
  left as-is. idleTimingPid left off (ETB idle = throttle, not timing).
- Idle PID gains (p=0.05 i=0.002, +-20% authority) left for on-car tuning;
  if idle sags under AC load raise i to 0.005-0.01.
- cltCrankingCorr is the manual-idle cranking position curve - unused with
  ETB, the 0.0 at -40C cell is harmless.
- After start verify against the reference: idle ~900, УОЗ 10-14 deg,
  MAP 38-41 kPa, TPS 1-4%; otherwise look for vacuum leaks / throttle stop.
